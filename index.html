How it works

For each category, the calculator divides the total marks obtained by the total marks available, then multiplies that percentage by the category weight. Assessments with more possible marks have more influence within their category.

Example: Tests 70% × 60%, quizzes 80% × 20%, assignments 90% × 20% = 76% semester mark.
